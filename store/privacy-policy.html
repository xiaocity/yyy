Privacy Policy — Sheep Meadow

Package com.cityg.caoyuan · Effective 21 August 2026

Sheep Meadow does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app requests no permissions at all — including no network permission — and makes no network requests while running. Everything it needs is contained in the installed package.

Information we collect

None. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. No information about you or your device is gathered, uploaded, or shared with anyone.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress in its own local storage on your device so that you can continue where you left off. This includes:

  • Level progress, stars and best times
  • In-game currency, items and upgrades
  • Collected sheep, achievements and ranch buildings
  • Settings such as language, sound and vibration
  • Your chosen display name, which you type yourself and which is never sent anywhere

This data never leaves your device. It is not readable by us or by anyone else.

Permissions

The app declares no Android permissions whatsoever. It cannot access the internet, your files, your location, your camera, your contacts, or any other part of your device. This can be verified from the installed package itself.

Third parties

There are no third-party services, servers, or SDKs. The app bundles the Font Awesome 6 Free icon font, which is embedded in the package and is never fetched over a network. Fonts are used under SIL OFL 1.1 and icons under CC BY 4.0; they are embedded unmodified. No data is sent to Fonticons, Inc. or anyone else.

Children

Sheep Meadow is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age. Because nothing is collected at all, there is no children's data for us to hold, use, or disclose.

Deleting your data

Because all data is local, you are in full control of it:

  • Use Me → Reset Progress inside the app to erase everything and start over.
  • Or clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, from your device settings.

Please note that there is no cloud backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data will permanently lose your progress, and it cannot be recovered by us.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever adds advertising, in-app purchases, network features, or anything else that changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

本应用不收集、不传输、不共享任何个人数据。

应用没有申请任何权限（包括网络权限），运行期间不发起任何网络请求。 它需要的一切都包含在安装包内。

我们收集的信息

没有。没有账号、没有登录、没有统计分析、没有广告、没有崩溃上报， 也没有任何第三方 SDK。不会收集、上传或向任何人共享关于你或你设备的任何信息。

保存在你设备上的信息

应用会把游戏进度保存在设备本机的存储里，以便你下次接着玩。包括：

  • 关卡进度、星级与最佳用时
  • 游戏内的金币、羊毛、道具与升级
  • 已收集的羊、成就与牧场建设
  • 语言、音效、震动等设置
  • 你自己填写的昵称 —— 它不会被发送到任何地方

这些数据从不离开你的设备，我们和任何第三方都读不到。

权限

应用没有声明任何 Android 权限。它无法访问网络、你的文件、位置、 摄像头、通讯录或设备的任何其它部分。这一点可以直接从安装包本身核实。

第三方

不存在任何第三方服务、服务器或 SDK。应用内嵌了 Font Awesome 6 Free 图标字体，它打包在安装包里，不会通过网络获取。 字体依 SIL OFL 1.1、图标依 CC BY 4.0 授权，仅嵌入使用、未作修改。 不会向 Fonticons, Inc. 或任何其他方发送数据。

儿童

本应用并非面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会在明知的情况下收集任何年龄段用户的信息。 由于根本不收集任何数据，因此不存在我们持有、使用或披露的儿童数据。

删除你的数据

所有数据都在本机，因此完全由你掌控：

  • 在应用内使用「我的 → 清空进度」，可以抹掉全部进度重新开始。
  • 或者在系统设置里清除应用数据，或直接卸载应用。

请注意：没有云端备份。卸载应用或清除数据会永久丢失进度，我们无法为你找回。

本政策的变更

如果将来某个版本引入广告、内购、联网功能，或其它改变数据处理方式的内容， 我们会在该版本发布前更新本政策，并修改上方的生效日期。
